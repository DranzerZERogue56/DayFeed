# DayFeed Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-23_

DayFeed is a notes app. It keeps everything on your phone. This page says
plainly what that means.

## The short version

- DayFeed has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.
- The app itself does not connect to the internet. It has no permission to.
- Nothing you write, record, photograph or save is sent to the people who make
  DayFeed, or to anyone else, by the app.

## What DayFeed stores, and where

Everything below stays on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- **Your notes** and the days, links and tags that go with them.
- **Photos** you take with the camera or pick from your gallery to attach to a
  note. DayFeed can also read the text out of a photo. That happens on your
  phone, not on a server.
- **Voice notes** you record, and the words typed out from them. Turning speech
  into text with the built-in option happens on your phone.
- **Reminders**, which are set on your phone and shown by your phone.
- **Vault entries** (labels, usernames and passwords you choose to save). These
  are locked with your phone's own secure storage, and the Vault asks for your
  fingerprint, face or screen lock each time you open it.

## Permissions, and why the app asks

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | To take photo notes. |
| Microphone | To record voice notes and to dictate. |
| Photos / files | To attach pictures you choose from your gallery. |
| Notifications | To show the reminders you set. |
| Fingerprint / face / screen lock | To unlock the Vault. |
| Run at start-up | So reminders you set still fire after the phone restarts. |
| Vibrate | Small buzzes when you tap certain buttons. |

DayFeed only asks when you use the feature that needs it, and you can turn any
of these off in your phone's settings.

## Dictation

When you speak a note, DayFeed turns your voice into text on your phone, using
a speech model that ships inside the app. The audio is not sent anywhere, and
the recording is deleted once the text is made. It works with no signal.

## Backups

Android can back up an app's data to your own Google account (this is a phone
setting, "Back up to Google Drive"). If that is on, your notes, photos and
voice notes may be included in that backup, and are restored if you move to a
new phone. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Google, not by
DayFeed. Vault passwords are not included. To stop it, turn off backup in your
phone's settings.

## Sharing and exports

DayFeed only sends something out of the app when you tell it to, for example by
exporting a note and choosing where to share it. What you send, and to whom, is
your choice.

## Children

DayFeed is a general notes app and is not aimed at children. It collects no
personal information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling DayFeed removes everything it stored on your phone. Nothing is
kept anywhere else, apart from a Google backup if you had one turned on, which
you can delete from your Google account.
